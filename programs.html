Programs

Care built for every
age and stage.

Seven dedicated classrooms, each designed around the specific developmental needs of children at that age. Full-time and year-round — because families need consistent, reliable care.

All Programs

From six weeks to kindergarten-ready.

6 weeks – 8 months

Infant A

Our youngest children receive one-on-one attentiveness in a calm, predictable environment. We follow each baby's individual schedule for feeding, sleeping, and play — because consistency builds trust, and trust builds everything else.

Small group size. Low ratios. Caregivers trained in infant development and responsive care.

8 – 15 months

Infant B

As babies begin to sit, crawl, pull up, and take their first steps, everything changes. Our Infant B classroom is designed for exploration — safe spaces to move, sensory materials to discover, and caregivers who celebrate every milestone.

Language development begins here: songs, narration, and responsive conversation are part of every hour.

15 – 23 months

Toddlers

Big strides in a little body. Toddlers are learning to walk confidently, use words, and assert their independence — all at once. Our toddler room is structured to support that energy with clear routines, gentle guidance, and lots of movement.

Staff-to-child ratios stay low so every child gets the attention they need during this fast-moving stage.

24 – 35 months

Twos

Language is exploding. So is personality. Two-year-olds are learning to communicate, cooperate, and navigate big feelings — with adult support they can count on. Our Twos classroom provides structure, warmth, and plenty of imaginative play.

We build vocabulary intentionally: books, storytelling, songs, and conversation throughout the day.

3 years

Preschool One

Three-year-olds are social beings ready to learn alongside peers. Preschool One focuses on cooperative play, early literacy, numbers, and the beginning of structured learning — all delivered with joy and patience.

Art, music, outdoor play, and circle time are woven into every day.

3 – 4 years

Preschool Two

Children in Preschool Two are building on what they've learned — longer attention spans, richer language, and the ability to work through problems with peers. We expand on early literacy and math in ways that feel like play.

Ready for more challenge. Still in an environment that's warm and supportive.

4 – 5 years

Pre-K

Our Pre-K classroom prepares children for kindergarten — not just with letters and numbers, but with the confidence, curiosity, and love of learning that makes school something to look forward to.

School-readiness skills: following directions, working in groups, expressing ideas, managing transitions. Children leave ready — and excited.

Tuition & Enrollment

Area average rates. Above-average care.

We're a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Every dollar we take in goes back into the program — to staffing, to the environment, and to keeping our rates accessible to working families. We keep our tuition at or below the Knoxville area average, without cutting corners on quality.

For current rates and enrollment availability, contact us directly. We're happy to answer your questions and schedule a tour so you can see the center for yourself.
