Our Foundation

Three decades of caring for Knoxville families.

Since 1995, the Davis Center has been a steady, loving place for children to learn, play, and grow. We're a non-profit, faith-based program — same neighborhood, same commitment, year after year.

Tuition that's fair for working families. Care that goes well beyond what fair tuition usually buys.

  • 30+ years of full-time, year-round care in Knoxville
  • 501(c)(3) faith-based non-profit — no shareholders, no franchise
  • At or below area average tuition — above-average care

Our Programs

Care designed for every stage of early childhood.

Seven dedicated classrooms — from infants at six weeks through pre-kindergarten. Each room is staffed and structured for that specific age and stage.

6 weeks – 8 months

Infant A

Gentle routines, responsive care, and a calm environment for the very youngest.

8 – 15 months

Infant B

Exploration and discovery as babies begin to move, reach, and find their voice.

15 – 23 months

Toddlers

Big steps for little people — walking, talking, and learning every minute.

24 – 35 months

Twos

Language, independence, and the beginning of imaginative play.

3 years

Preschool One

Building social skills, curiosity, and early learning foundations.

3 – 4 years

Preschool Two

Expanding language, creativity, and cooperative play.

4 – 5 years

Pre-K

School-readiness skills taught with joy — numbers, letters, and building confidence.
